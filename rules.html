Faro

  • 52‑card deck; suits ignored; ranks A–K.
  • Before each coup, place bets on ranks (A–K). Coppering a bet wagers against that rank.
  • Each coup reveals two cards: bank then player.
  • Bank card: uncoppered bets on that rank lose; coppered bets win even money.
  • Player card: uncoppered bets on that rank win even money; coppered bets lose.
  • Doublet (same rank twice): house takes half of all bets on that rank.

Poker

Texas Hold’em with blinds and standard hand ranking. Join a table, take a seat, ready up, and act when it’s your turn. Pots resolve at showdown or on fold.

Hazard

Choose a main (e.g., 7). Roll two dice; outcomes follow traditional Hazard rules. Wagers resolve immediately on certain totals, otherwise a point is established and rolls continue until point or main resolves.

Shell Game

  • Pick one of three shells; contract randomly selects the winning cup.
  • If your guess matches the winning cup, you win 2× wager (bankroll permitting); otherwise you lose the wager.

Dak & Chog (Coin Flip)

  • Pick Dak or Chog and place a wager.
  • Contract flips; matching your choice pays 2× wager (bankroll permitting); otherwise you lose the wager.
