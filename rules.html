Faro

  • Deck: 52-card deck, suits ignored. Ranks are A (1) through K (13).
  • Betting: Place any number of bets on ranks. Toggle “Copper” to bet against a rank.
  • Coup (deal): Two cards are drawn face up each round — first the Bank card, then the Player card.
  • Resolution: Bank card → bets on that rank lose; coppered bets on that rank win even money. Player card → bets on that rank win even money; coppered bets on that rank lose.
  • Doublet: If both cards are the same rank, the house takes half of all stakes on that rank; no wins are paid on that rank for that coup.
  • Payouts: Even money (1:1). Winning bets return stake plus winnings; losing bets forfeit stake.
  • Multiple bets: You may place multiple ranks per coup. Bets lock when the coup begins.
  • Limits: Min/max bet may apply; payouts are capped by the game bankroll.

Poker (No-Limit Texas Hold'em)

  • Seats: Up to 8 players. A dealer button rotates clockwise each hand.
  • Blinds: Small blind (SB) and big blind (BB) are auto-posted by the two seats left of the button.
  • Deal: Each active player receives 2 private hole cards.
  • Betting rounds: Preflop → Flop (3 community cards) → Turn (1) → River (1).
  • Actions: Fold, Check (if no bet), Call, Bet/Raise (min bet is BB; min raise equals the previous raise size). All-in allowed; side pots supported.
  • Showdown: Best 5-card hand using any combination of hole and community cards wins the pot. Ties split evenly.
  • Hand ranking (high → low): Royal Flush; Straight Flush; Four of a Kind; Full House; Flush; Straight; Three of a Kind; Two Pair; One Pair; High Card.
  • Timing: If a player does not act before the timer expires, the table may auto-fold/check.
  • Buy-in/Cash-out: Uses DCMon. Approvals wrap/unwrap MON as needed.
  • Rake: A small rake may be taken from settled pots (displayed in-game when applicable).

Hazard (Dice)

  • Main: Choose a main (5–9 inclusive). Default is 7.
  • Come-out roll: - Roll equals main → WIN. - Roll of 2 or 3 → LOSS. - Roll of 11 or 12 → If main is 5 or 9, WIN; if main is 7, LOSS; otherwise LOSS.
  • Point phase: If the come-out is 4,5,6,8,9,10 (and not an immediate win/loss), that total becomes the point. Keep rolling: - Hit the point again before rolling the main → WIN. - Roll the main before the point → LOSS.
  • Bets: Even money. Each wager settles on the same sequence of rolls.

Shell Game

  • Three shells, one hidden token. Pick a shell; the contract reveals the result.
  • Payouts: Correct guess pays 2× (stake returned + winnings), subject to bankroll limits.

Dak & Chog (Coin Flip)

  • Pick Dak or Chog, then place a wager.
  • Payouts: Correct call pays 2× (stake returned + winnings), subject to bankroll limits.
