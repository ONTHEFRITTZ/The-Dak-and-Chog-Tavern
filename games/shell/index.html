Shell Game Rules

  • Choose a shell (left, center, or right).
  • Place your wager and the contract selects a winning cup.
  • If your guess matches the winning cup: you win 2x wager (bankroll permitting).
  • Otherwise you lose your wager.
  • Gas fees apply to each play.
